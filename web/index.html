      FAQ
      
            
      The questions every partner asks.
      
          
      
          
      
            
      
              
      How can an AI system legally touch HIPAA & PHI data?
      
              
      Serverless Mistral OCR (/v1/ocr) and Fireworks AI operate under an executed Enterprise HIPAA BAA (contractual; ZDR documented) with enforced Zero Data Retention (ZDR). A hard CRM boundary permits only de-identified keys and dollar math to leave the HIPAA boundary.
      
              
      Mistral ZDR Fireworks data handling Mistral Trust Center Fireworks HIPAA support 
      
            
      
            
      
              
      How does it read messy 300-page hospital bills & policies—and why Markdown?
      
              
      Mistral OCR 4.1 vision handles scans and layout noise. Markdown matches LLM pretraining, strips layout noise, cuts tokens, and improves extraction accuracy.
      
              
      Mistral OCR 4.1 model docs 
      
            
      
            
      
              
      How does the AI actually know ERISA & Stop-Loss Law without hallucinating?
      
              
      Top-percentile legal reasoning AI models are paired with specialized legal and clinical verification tools connected directly to authoritative sources like CourtListener , eCFR Title 29 , CMS NCD 110.24 , and openFDA . Every conclusion carries an exact citation.
      
            
      
            
      
              
      Why pay per token on Fireworks AI instead of buying GPUs or hiring 10 more analysts?
      
              
      If you rent or buy dedicated GPUs to self-host models, those GPUs sit idle 95% of the time while you pay for them around the clock. By using HIPAA-compliant serverless APIs on Fireworks AI , you only pay for compute at the exact moment an analyst is actively processing a claim packet—giving you access to much smarter frontier models at a fraction of the cost of dedicated hardware.
      
              
      Fireworks serverless pricing 
      
            
      
          
      
          
      Production deployment operates under an Enterprise HIPAA BAA (contractual) + enforced Zero Data Retention (ZDR) , documented across all inference and OCR endpoints.
