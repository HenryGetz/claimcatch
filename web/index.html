      FAQ

      The questions every partner asks.

      How can an AI system legally touch HIPAA & PHI data?

      ZDR + BAA cover Mistral OCR inline /v1/ocr (ZDR requested per call) and Fireworks store:false. A hard CRM boundary permits only de-identified keys and dollar math to leave the VPC.

      How does it read messy 300-page hospital bills & policies—and why Markdown?

      Mistral OCR vision handles scans and layout noise. Markdown matches LLM pretraining, strips layout noise, cuts tokens, and improves extraction accuracy.

      How does the AI actually know ERISA & Stop-Loss Law without hallucinating?

      Top-percentile legal reasoning is paired with deterministic FastMCP calls to CourtListener, eCFR Title 29, CMS NCD 110.24, and openFDA. Every conclusion carries an exact citation.

      Why pay per token on Fireworks AI instead of buying GPUs or hiring 10 more analysts?

      No idle H100 waste. Serverless inference costs roughly $6 per 1,000 pages, parses 312 pages in 12 seconds, and lands under $2 per claim.

      BAA and ZDR scope confirmed contractually; vendor public documentation does not publish a BAA.
